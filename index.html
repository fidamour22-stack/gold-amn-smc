How the analyzer checks the setup

This prototype uses recent candle swing levels as a mechanical approximation: a sweep is a wick beyond a prior local high/low followed by a close back inside; MSS is a close beyond a recent opposing swing; 1M confirmation checks for a directional candle after the sequence. It is not a full discretionary SMC interpretation. A valid signal requires the sequence in order: sweep → MSS → 1M confirmation, with 4H and 15M not contradicting the trade.
